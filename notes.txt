Groq is a hardware and inference company known for extremely fast LLM serving
using their LPU (Language Processing Unit) chips. Unlike GPUs, LPUs are
designed specifically for the sequential, memory-bound nature of transformer
inference, which lets Groq serve open models like Llama and Mixtral at very
high tokens-per-second rates with low latency. Groq offers an OpenAI-style
chat completions API, so existing code that talks to similar APIs can often
be adapted with minimal changes. Pricing is usage-based and per-token, and
free-tier API keys are available for testing and small projects.
